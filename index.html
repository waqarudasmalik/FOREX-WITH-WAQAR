ICT Trade Entry Rules — Trigger / SL / TP

In 8 ICT strategies mein har concept ki visual chart, easy explanation, condition checklist, aur exact Trigger, Stop Loss (SL), aur Take Profit (TP) rules diye gaye hain. Bina in tamam conditions ke entry na lein.

1. Fair Value Gap (FVG) / Imbalance

3-candle imbalance jahan Candle 1 ki wick aur Candle 3 ki wick overlap nahi karti — market isay wapis "fill" karne aati hai.
Easy Explanation:

FVG 3 candles ka aisa pattern hai jahan beech wali candle bohot tezi se banti hai aur pehli/teesri candle ki wicks aapas mein touch nahi hotin. Ye khaali jagah (Imbalance) market ko wapis kheenchti hai — jab price is zone ke 50% level (CE — Consequent Encroachment) tak wapis aa kar reject hoti hai, wahan se trend ki direction mein entry milti hai.

  • 3-candle pattern jahan Candle 1 aur Candle 3 ki wicks ke darmiyan clear gap ho
  • FVG higher-timeframe bias/trend ke sath align ho
  • Price ka wapis FVG ke 50% (CE) tak aana aur LTF reversal candle dena
Trigger
FVG ka CE (50%) retest + LTF confirmation
Stop Loss (SL)
FVG ke far edge (Candle 1 wick) se thoda peeche
Take Profit (TP)
Next liquidity pool / pichla swing high-low

2. Institutional Order Block (OB)

Woh last opposite-direction candle jiske baad institutions ne strong impulsive move launch ki.
Easy Explanation:

Order Block woh aakhri opposite-colour candle hoti hai jiske baad market ne bohot tezi se ek direction mein move kiya. Ye zone banks/institutions ki entry ka area samjha jata hai. Jab price wapis is zone par aati hai, wahan se dobara strong move ki umeed hoti hai.

  • OB se pehle clear impulsive move (BOS ya CHoCH) honi chahiye
  • OB ideally kisi FVG ya liquidity sweep se juda ho (confluence)
  • Retest par LTF CHoCH/BOS confirmation dekhein
Trigger
OB retest par LTF CHoCH/BOS confirmation
Stop Loss (SL)
OB ke high/low se thoda peeche (invalidation wick)
Take Profit (TP)
Next opposing OB / liquidity pool

3. Liquidity Sweeps (Buy-side & Sell-side)

Market pehle stop-losses/pending orders (liquidity) ko "sweep" (wick se todti) karti hai, phir asli direction mein move karti hai.
Easy Explanation:

Equal highs ya equal lows ke pass retail traders ke stop-losses aur pending orders (liquidity) jama hote hain. Smart money price ko thodi der ke liye us level ke paar dhaka (wick) deti hai taake liquidity le sake, phir turant reverse ho kar asli direction mein move karti hai. Ye sweep hi hamare liye entry ka signal hota hai.

  • Price ka clear equal highs/lows ko wick se todna (sweep)
  • Sweep ke turant baad strong rejection candle close
  • Sweep higher-timeframe draw-on-liquidity ke sath align ho
Trigger
Sweep ke baad reversal candle close + LTF CHoCH
Stop Loss (SL)
Sweep wick ke extreme se thoda peeche
Take Profit (TP)
Opposite side liquidity (equal highs/lows)

4. Market Structure Shift (MSS) / Break of Structure (BOS)

Jab price kisi important swing high/low ko candle-close ke sath tor deti hai, market structure shift ho jati hai.
Easy Explanation:

Jab market apni pichli trend-line structure (pichla swing high ya low) ko candle-close ke sath tor deti hai, isay Break of Structure (BOS) ya Market Structure Shift (MSS) kehte hain. Ye confirm karta hai ke short-term trend ab reverse ho chuka hai ya continue ho raha hai — is break ke baad bane FVG ya OB par hum entry dhoondte hain.

  • Candle body ka close pichle swing point se saaf upar/neeche hona chahiye
  • Break higher-timeframe bias ke sath match kare (confluence)
  • Break ke baad bana FVG/OB entry ke liye use karein
Trigger
Candle close swing point se paar + retest of break zone
Stop Loss (SL)
Toote gaye swing point se thoda peeche
Take Profit (TP)
Next major structural level / draw on liquidity

5. Optimal Trade Entry (OTE)

Impulsive leg ke 61.8% – 78.6% Fibonacci retracement zone — institutional "discount/premium" entry area.
Easy Explanation:

Jab market ek strong impulsive leg banaye, us leg par Fibonacci lagane se 61.8% se 78.6% ke darmiyan wali zone "Optimal Trade Entry" kehlati hai. Ye woh area hai jahan se institutions discount (buy side) ya premium (sell side) par apni positions add karte hain. Isi zone ke retest par best risk-reward wali entry milti hai.

  • Ek clear, strong impulsive leg (BOS ke sath) honi chahiye
  • Fibonacci ko sahi swing high se swing low tak lagayein
  • OTE zone (61.8%-78.6%) mein LTF confirmation ka wait karein
Trigger
61.8%-78.6% fib zone mein LTF reversal confirmation
Stop Loss (SL)
79%-88.6% fib extension / swing point se peeche
Take Profit (TP)
1:2 se 1:4 measured move / pichli structure

6. ICT Silver Bullet Strategy

10:00-11:00 AM New York time ka high-probability killzone window — sweep + FVG combo entry.
Easy Explanation:

Silver Bullet ek time-based ICT strategy hai jo sirf 10:00-11:00 AM New York session ke ek-ghante ke window mein trade hoti hai. Is window mein pehle ek liquidity sweep hoti hai, phir usi move mein ek FVG banta hai. Us FVG ke retest par hi entry li jati hai — bahar is window ke ye strategy trade nahi ki jati.

  • Sirf 10:00-11:00 AM New York time window mein dekhein
  • Window ke start mein liquidity sweep honi chahiye
  • Sweep ke turant baad FVG banna aur usi FVG ka retest confirm karein
Trigger
Killzone mein sweep ke baad FVG retest
Stop Loss (SL)
FVG / sweep wick ke extreme se thoda peeche
Take Profit (TP)
Same-session opposing liquidity

7. Balanced Price Range (BPR)

Do opposite-direction FVGs jo ek hi price zone par overlap karte hain — imbalance yahan "balanced" ho jati hai.
Easy Explanation:

BPR tab banta hai jab market ek direction mein tezi se move kare (ek FVG banaye) aur foran wapis reverse ho kar dusri direction mein bhi tezi se move kare (dusra opposite FVG banaye). Jab dono opposite FVGs ek hi price zone par overlap karte hain, woh area "balanced" ban jata hai — market aksar wapis isi overlap zone ko retest karti hai jahan se clean, high-probability entry milti hai.

  • Do opposite-direction impulsive moves ek hi price area mein overlap karni chahiye
  • Overlap wali zone ko clearly BPR ke tor par mark karein
  • Price ka wapis is zone mein aana aur reject hona confirm karein
Trigger
BPR overlap zone retest + rejection candle
Stop Loss (SL)
BPR zone ke outer edge se thoda peeche
Take Profit (TP)
Next liquidity draw

8. Inverted Fair Value Gap (IFVG)

Jab ek FVG candle-close ke sath tootti hai, wahi zone opposite direction ke liye support/resistance ban jati hai.
Easy Explanation:

IFVG tab banta hai jab market kisi valid FVG ki izzat nahi karti aur use candle-close ke sath tez tor deti hai. Woh purana gap ab ulta kaam karne lagta hai — jo pehle buying (support) ka kaam kar raha tha, ab price ke niche tootne ke baad wahi zone selling (resistance) ka kaam karega, aur wapis touch hone par selling ki solid entry milti hai.

  • Purana FVG candle body ke close hone ke sath cleanly tootna chahiye
  • Price ka usi zone par wapis aana aur reverse direction mein reject hona lazmi hai
  • LTF confirmation ke bagair entry na lein
Trigger
Broken FVG zone retest + reverse-direction rejection
Stop Loss (SL)
Broken FVG ke far edge se thoda peeche
Take Profit (TP)
Next liquidity pool

ICT / SMC Strategy Confirmation Guide

Har concept ke sath uski visual image aur simple explanation di gayi hai. Jab tak checklist ki sab conditions pass na hon, tab tak entry bilkul na lein.

1. Order Block (Bullish / Bearish)

Woh last opposite-direction candle jiske baad strong impulsive move aayi.
Easy Explanation:

Jab market tezi se upar jati hai, to shuruat ki aakhri laal (bearish) candle ko hum Bullish Order Block kehte hain. Bank aur institutions isi area se badi buying karte hain. Jab price ghumm kar wapis isi zone par aati hai (Retest karti hai), to wahan se market dobara bohot tezi se upar uchalti hai. Hamein isi retest par apni buying entry plan karni hoti hai.

  • Order Block se pehle ek clear strong impulsive move (BOS ya CHoCH) honi chahiye
  • Price wapis usi Order Block zone mein aani chahiye (retest)
  • Retest par lower timeframe confirmation (chhota BOS/CHoCH) dekhein

2. Fair Value Gap (FVG)

3-candle pattern jahan beech wali candle ki wick pehli aur teesri candle se overlap na kare.
Easy Explanation:

Fair Value Gap (FVG) market mein 3 candles ka ek aisa pattern hota hai jahan beech wali کینڈل bohot badi aur tezi se banti hai. Isme Pehli candle ki high (wick) aur Teesri candle ki low (wick) aapas mein touch nahi hotin, jisse beech mein ek khaali jagah (Imbalance) reh jati hai. Market hamesha is khaali jagah ko magnet ki tarah bharne (Fill karne) wapis aati hai, aur jaise hi market is gap mein enter ho, hum trend ki direction mein trade le sakte hain.

  • Candle 1 ki wick aur Candle 3 ki wick ke darmiyan saaf gap (khali space) hona chahiye
  • Price ka wapis is FVG zone ke andar aane aur reject hone ka wait karein

3. Inverse Fair Value Gap (IFVG)

Jab ek FVG break/close ho jaye, to wahi zone opposite direction ke liye support/resistance ban jati hai.
Easy Explanation:

Inverse FVG tab banta hai jab market kisi valid FVG ki izzat nahi karti aur use tezi se tod kar (candle close ke sath) nikal jati hai. To woh purana gap ab ulta kaam karne lagta hai. Misal ke taur par, jo gap pehle buying (Support) ka kaam kar raha tha, ab price ke niche tootne ke baad wahi zone selling (Resistance) ka kaam karega. Jab market wapis upar us zone ko touch karegi, to wahan se selling ki solid trade milti hai.

  • Purana FVG candle body ke close hone ke sath cleanly tootna chahiye
  • Price ka usi zone par wapis aana aur reverse direction mein reject hona lazmi hai

4. Balanced Price Range (BPR)

Do overlapping FVGs (ek bullish, ek bearish) jo same price zone mein bante hain.
Easy Explanation:

Balanced Price Range (BPR) tab banta hai jab market pehle aik direction mein tezi se move kare (ek FVG banaye) aur foran wapis reverse ho kar dusri direction mein bhi tezi se move kare (dusra opposite FVG banaye). Jab dono opposite FVGs ek hi price zone par overlap karte hain, to woh area "balanced" ban jata hai — kyunke dono directions ka imbalance yahan ek dusre ko cancel kar dete hain. Market aksar wapis isi overlap zone ko retest karti hai, aur wahan se ek clean, high-probability entry milti hai.

  • Do opposite-direction impulsive moves ek hi price area mein overlap karni chahiye
  • Overlap wali zone ko clearly BPR ke tor par mark karein
  • Price ka wapis is zone mein aana aur reject hona confirm karein
